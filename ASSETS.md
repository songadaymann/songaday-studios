## Fumble stories and paintings

Stories on songaday.studio/fumble are written by anonymous visitors, and their paintings are
generated by OpenAI's image model through Codex. Both live in Cloudflare (the `songaday-fumble`
D1 database and R2 bucket), not in this repository. Local copies of paintings go to the ignored
`output/fumble/`.

## Running without the private bundle

`node build.mjs` and `node build-production.mjs` use the site's hosted media when
local `assets` is absent. They do not download or redistribute that media.
Replace these references with your own assets for an independent deployment.

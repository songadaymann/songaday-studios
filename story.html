Song A Day has never fit neatly into any business model.

Commissions used to be a big part of my income, but with the advent of AI-generated music, they've mostly dried up.

Through all these 18 years, I've had one dream: make enough money that I can focus solely on what I do best.

Every Song A Day song lives under a Creative Commons license. Anyone is free to cover, remix, and use it — part of Song A Day's life as a public good. Learn about Creative Commons ↗

Patronage

I've tried Patreon, paid YouTube subscribers, and a Bandcamp fan club. Until now, none has made up more than one-sixteenth of my income.

Recently, C.Y. Lee — an early Microsoft and RealPlayer employee — became a friend and mentor. He now uses his resources to fund dozens of artists across the country.

I'm fiscally sponsored by Fridays at The Park, an LA recording collective and nonprofit. That means every donation is tax-deductible.

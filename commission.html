Get a song written for you

I’ve written a song every day since January 1, 2009. I’ll write one for you: for a birthday, a wedding, an anniversary, a launch, or just because.

How it works

  1. Tell me about it. The more information the better.
  2. You get your place in line. I write songs in the order they come in, and I’ll email you when you move up.
  3. I write it. When it’s done you get a private page where you can listen and read the lyrics.
  4. You pay what you want, and then you can download it.

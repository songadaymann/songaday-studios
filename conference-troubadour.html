How it works

  1. I watch every talk at your meeting or conference, taking lots of notes.
  2. At the end of the day I have a short song ready, complete with a catchy chorus to get everyone singing along.
  3. I have the audience film me, then cut together all of their footage so that they can always come back to the song later.
  4. Your attendees leave energized and happy, the message of your conference (often literally) stuck in their head.

Where I’ve played

From 2013 to 2019, I primarily made my living on the conference circuit. I’d watch all the talks, typing furiously on my laptop taking notes. Then, during breaks, I’d turn all those notes into a catchy song. At the end of the day, I’d perform the “recap song”, with the audience singing the chorus; it was always a great way to end a conference.
